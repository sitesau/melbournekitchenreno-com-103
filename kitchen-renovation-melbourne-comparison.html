Kitchen Renovation Melbourne Comparison Explained

When comparing kitchen renovation melbourne options, the first step is understanding the cost ranges. A budget refresh typically costs between $12,000 and $30,000. This involves changing door fronts, benchtops and splashback without altering the layout. A full renovation usually sits between $25,000 and $60,000. This includes new cabinetry, plumbing and electrical updates, splashback and soft close hardware. Premium renovations can exceed $80,000 when natural stone, custom joinery or a butler's pantry are included. The kitchen renovation melbourne guide often highlights this distinction to help homeowners align their expectations with the scope of work.

It is important to note that one proposal may assume the existing footprint stays in place, while another includes demolition, manufacture and installation for a full rebuild. Asking each renovator to price the same written scope is usually more useful than debating whether one total looks high or low in isolation. State whether the layout stays as is or changes. List the finishes you already know you want. Note whether the home is a period property or an apartment. Ask for the quote in fixed price written form.

Timeline breakdown for your project

Timing is another critical factor when planning your project. A mid range full renovation generally takes 6 to 12 weeks from contract signing to handover. The front half of this period is dominated by cabinetry and stone fabrication, which takes around 4 to 6 weeks. On site work typically runs for 2 to 4 weeks, followed by 1 to 2 weeks of finishing. If a quote suggests a shorter timeline, ask whether appliances have been selected and if cabinetry production has been accounted for. The kitchen renovation melbourne guide notes that lead times on cabinets and stone drive the schedule.

For a budget refresh, the timeline is often shorter because demolition and structural changes are minimal. However, cabinetry and stone fabrication still require time. The key is to align your expectations with the lead times of materials. Confirm whether the renovator has allocated sufficient time for each stage of the project before signing any agreement.

Project type considerations

Project type significantly influences both cost and design. Period terraces, Edwardians and bungalows across Melbourne suit made to measure joinery rather than a flat pack run. The design discussion focuses on finish style and how cabinetry sits with existing character. For apartment work, the guide flags owners corporation documentation, acoustic underlay and clever storage as real parts of the job. These details are worth raising at the first visit because they can affect what is practical and what needs approval.

Compact kitchens in apartments require careful planning to maximise storage and bench space. Owners corporation documentation often dictates specific requirements for acoustic underlay and materials. A renovator experienced in apartment work will understand these constraints and can advise on the best approach. For period homes, the focus is often on preserving character while improving storage and function. The right renovator will balance these competing demands to deliver a result that is both character true and genuinely functional.

What to check in a contract

The process for securing a renovator involves four main steps. First, submit the form with a few details about your kitchen, suburb, scope and rough budget. Second, a VBA-registered renovator reviews your project size, style and timeline, then contacts you to discuss the brief. Third, the renovator visits for a free in-home design consult to take measurements and prepare a fixed price written quote. Fourth, the build and handover phase begins with a fixed price written contract and a transparent variations process.

When reviewing the contract, pay close attention to the inclusions and exclusions. Ensure that all materials, such as benchtops and hardware, are clearly specified. Check whether the contract includes domestic building insurance where the contract value requires it. A transparent variations process is also essential, as it outlines how any changes to the scope will be handled and priced. This protects you from unexpected costs and ensures the project stays on track.

Hardware and finish standards

Hardware is another useful checkpoint when comparing renovation quotes. The FAQ says most quality projects include soft close runners and hinges, such as Blum or Hettich, as standard. That makes it worth checking the specified brand and whether soft close is built into the base price. If you need an early estimate before the visit, the published calculator is presented as an indicative range with cost driver method and assumptions shown on the page, which makes it a planning tool rather than a substitute for a written quote.

Define the scope before requesting a quote. Write down layout intent, rough budget, home type and must keep features. This preparation ensures the renovator provides an accurate and comparable quote. It also saves time during the design consult by focusing the discussion on the most important aspects of the project.

Common questions

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Melbourne? Most Melbourne kitchen renovations land between $25,000 and $60,000 fully fitted. A budget refresh typically starts around $12,000 to $30,000, while premium renovations with natural stone and custom joinery can exceed $80,000. Figures are indicative only - the renovator you engage provides a written fixed price quote.

How long does a kitchen renovation take in Melbourne? Allow roughly 6 to 12 weeks from contract signing to handover for a mid range full renovation. Cabinetry and stone fabrication take around 4 to 6 weeks as a lead-in, on-site work runs 2 to 4 weeks, and finishing adds 1 to 2 weeks. Lead times on cabinets and stone drive the front half of the project.

What is a butler's pantry and is it worth it? A butler's pantry is a secondary prep and storage room behind the main kitchen, often with a second sink and bench. It keeps the main kitchen clear for entertaining and is one of the most requested features on larger Melbourne renovations. Whether it suits depends on your floor plan and budget.
